100 Thieves launches its first skin bundle for R6, The Exiled. Available now in the R6 in-game store, the drop marks a milestone moment for the organization: its debut cosmetic in one of the most storied tactical shooters in esports, and the first of many to come as an official R6 Share partner. The Exiled bundle transforms the operator into a towering, supernatural knight who has been waiting in a ruined throne room for a challenger worthy of the fight.
