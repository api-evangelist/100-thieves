Introducing the PSA + 100 Thieves Collection, a limited 5-piece capsule designed with collectors in mind, available exclusively at San Diego Comic-Con from July 23rd through July 26th, 2026. Created in collaboration with PSA, the most trusted name in trading card grading, the collection features a hoodie, a tee, slab bags, and exclusive accessories — built to represent the hobby wherever you take it. From the show floor to the mail day, every piece is made for collectors and first-timers alike.
