Introducing the Pokémon GO Fest + 100 Thieves Collection, a limited-edition capsule inspired by Mega Mewtwo X and Mega Mewtwo Y, available exclusively in Chicago during Pokémon GO Fest starting May 30th till June 7th, 2026. Created in collaboration with Pokémon GO, the collection features a hat, shirt, and hoodie, with each item available in two versions: one featuring Mega Mewtwo X and the other featuring Mega Mewtwo Y. With every hoodie purchase, Trainers will receive a voucher code to redeem a matching in-game avatar item.
